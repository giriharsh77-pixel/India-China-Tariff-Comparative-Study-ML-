# Tariff Advantage and Export Market Selection
### A comparative India–China trade analysis using machine learning
*Prospect Selection project — Machine Learning for Business Analytics, Plaksha University MS-AI, Monsoon 2026*

**Decision.** An Indian export-promotion organisation can support only **K = 10** destination-market × HS6-product
opportunities a year. Which ones should it back? We rank ~2,000 possible pairs a year (10 markets × 200 products) by the
expected **next-year gain in India's share of the market's imports**, comparing a **historical export-momentum baseline**
with a **machine-learning ranking** that also uses the **tariff advantage** India has (or lacks) relative to China.

**Headline results** (rolling-origin backtest, held-out decision years 2021–2024, outcomes 2022–2025, K = 10):

| Metric | Historical baseline | ML model | ML without tariffs | Random pick |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Precision@10 (share of picks where India's share rose) | 33% | 55% | 60% | 54% |
| Average future market-share change@10 (pp) | -6.47 | -0.93 | +0.14 | +0.02 |
| Average future export growth@10 (log) | -1.67 | -0.66 | -0.55 | -0.02 |
| NDCG@10 | 0.112 | 0.123 | 0.137 | 0.045 |

Momentum is worse than a random pick (fast growers mean-revert); ML avoids that trap but beats random only narrowly overall
(it underperformed in the 2024→2025 fold, the year of the US tariff shock), and tariff features add no measurable predictive value.

Dataset: 12,802 candidate rows (10 markets × 195 HS6 products,
decision years 2015–2025). Selected model: RF.
Live 2025 shortlists: `outputs/rankings/top10_ml_2025.csv` and `top10_baseline_2025.csv`. All numbers are regenerated by the pipeline.

## 3. Design in one paragraph

Tariffs are those **imposed by the importing market** on Indian-origin and Chinese-origin goods (WITS/UNCTAD TRAINS; the
origin-specific preferential rate if reported, otherwise MFN — the same rule for both origins). **Tariff advantage = tariff on
China − tariff on India.** Trade data are **importer-reported** UN Comtrade imports from India, China and the world, 2012–2025;
the **EU-27 is one customs territory**. The product universe (200 HS6 codes continuous across HS2012/2017/2022) is fixed using
**2014 data only**. Candidates at decision year *t* must already have ≥ US$1m of Indian exports and ≥ US$20m of market imports.
The **outcome** is the change in India's import share from *t* to *t+1*; success = share rises. The **baseline** ranks by India's
3-year export growth; the **ML model** (gradient boosting vs random forest, chosen on validation years 2018–2020) predicts the
share change from 33 decision-time features. Both are evaluated on held-out decision years 2021–2024 (outcomes 2022–2025)
with the **same candidates and K**, using Precision@K, mean realised share change, export growth and NDCG@K. A live 2025
shortlist is produced for the next promotion cycle.

## 5. Key limitations (see `docs/limitations.md`)

* US Section 301 / 2025 duties on China and unilateral GSP preferences are **not** in the public TRAINS data → the US, EU, UK
  and Canada show a measured advantage of zero.
* TRAINS has no preferential schedules after 2021 → last observed rates carried forward and flagged.
* Associations, not causal effects; the simulation is a sensitivity analysis of the model.
